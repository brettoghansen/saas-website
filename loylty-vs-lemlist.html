Loylty vs Lemlist: Adding the Channel Cold Email Can't Reach

Lemlist is a personalized email outreach platform with a strong reputation for high-quality sequences. Loylty is a contact form outreach platform that reaches businesses through their own website forms. They're not competitors — they're different channels. But knowing when each delivers better results is what separates strategic outreach from random activity.

TL;DR

Lemlist is a cold email platform known for personalized sequences, image personalization, video messaging, and strong deliverability features including its own warm-up network (Lemwarm). It's a mature, feature-rich tool for teams that run email outreach as a primary channel. Loylty submits outreach through business contact forms — zero spam filters, zero domain infrastructure, and mandatory human review before every submission. They solve different problems within the same goal: reaching businesses that don't know you yet.

Deep Dive

What Lemlist Actually Does

Lemlist is a cold email platform that's differentiated itself through personalization features — particularly image personalization (dynamic images with the prospect's name, company logo, or custom text inserted directly into the image) and video messages embedded in emails. These aren't gimmicks; they produce measurably higher reply rates in the right contexts.

Its Lemwarm service handles domain warmup and reputation monitoring automatically. The sequence builder supports multi-step campaigns with conditional branching and A/B testing — you can set different follow-up paths based on whether a prospect opened your email, clicked a link, or replied. It also supports basic LinkedIn touchpoints within sequences for a multi-channel approach.

What Lemlist is genuinely excellent at:

  • Image and video personalization — dynamic personalization that stands out in crowded inboxes
  • Multi-step sequences — sophisticated follow-up logic with conditions and A/B testing built in
  • Domain warmup — Lemwarm handles sending reputation automatically
  • Community and education — strong user community with templates, playbooks, and training
  • Analytics — detailed open, click, and reply tracking across sequences

Honest limitations: Lemlist is subject to spam filters like all cold email. Even with warmup, deliverability fluctuates — especially as inbox providers tighten filters. Video personalization is compelling but adds production overhead. Pricing scales for larger teams. Its prospect database is limited compared to dedicated prospecting tools, so most users source their own lists.

Deep Dive

What Loylty Actually Does

Loylty is a contact form outreach platform. Instead of sending cold emails, it submits outreach messages through the contact forms on business websites — the same form any customer would use to reach out. This means your message lands in the business's website inquiry inbox, not their email inbox, and spam filters don't apply.

Here's how a Loylty campaign works: you select businesses from Loylty's database of 2.3M+ SMBs (filterable by industry, location, and more) or upload a CSV of business URLs. Loylty's crawler visits each website, detects the contact form, and maps the relevant fields. Its AI generates a personalized outreach message for each business — tailored to what they do and who you're reaching. Before anything sends, every submission goes into a mandatory human review queue. You (or your team) approve each one before it goes out.

What Loylty is genuinely excellent at:

  • Zero spam filter exposure — contact form submissions reach the website inquiry inbox, not the email spam folder
  • No infrastructure overhead — no domains to warm up, no sending reputation to manage
  • Mandatory pre-send review — built-in quality gate that cold email platforms don't require
  • Built-in SMB database — 2.3M+ businesses ready to target without a separate prospecting tool
  • Fast setup — same-day campaign launch, no warmup period required

Honest limitations: Loylty is a single-channel, single-submission tool. There are no multi-step sequences — one submission per business per campaign. No image or video personalization. Not designed for enterprise sales where individual named contact data drives the outreach. Best suited for SMB and local business targeting at volume.

Decision Guide

When to Choose Each Tool

Choose Lemlist when...

You're running sophisticated email sequences and want personalization features that stand out in the inbox.

  • You need multi-step sequences with conditional branching and A/B testing
  • You want image or video personalization to increase reply rates
  • You're sending high volume across multiple email accounts
  • You need detailed email analytics (opens, clicks, replies) per sequence
  • Your targets have known email addresses and you're doing outbound email as a primary channel

Choose Loylty when...

You want zero spam filter exposure and a channel that reaches businesses through their own inquiry inbox.

  • You want to reach businesses through their website contact forms
  • Your email deliverability is struggling and you need a supplemental channel
  • You're targeting local businesses and SMBs at volume
  • You want mandatory pre-send human review on every message
  • You want to add a second channel to your Lemlist campaigns without extra domain infrastructure
The strongest case for using both together: Run your Lemlist sequence to your email list simultaneously with a Loylty contact form campaign to the same businesses — with a different sender identity and message framing. Different channel, same prospects, higher total reach. When a prospect sees your name in their email inbox via Lemlist and also receives a contact form inquiry on their website via Loylty, it reinforces your outreach in a way that neither channel achieves alone. You're not doubling down on the same channel — you're covering two completely separate inboxes.
