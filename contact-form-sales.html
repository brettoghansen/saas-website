The Problem

The Modern B2B Sales Stack Problem

Sales teams in 2026 are running the same three-channel playbook that everyone ran in 2020 — cold email, LinkedIn, and phone calls — and competing for attention in an increasingly crowded space. The channels haven't changed, but the competitive density in each one has gotten dramatically worse.

Cold email deliverability has deteriorated every year. Google and Microsoft have aggressively tightened spam filters. New DMARC and DKIM requirements have made it harder to operate sending infrastructure. Deliverability rates that were 90% in 2021 are now 60–70% for many domains. More email is going to spam before it's ever seen.

LinkedIn InMail is expensive and response rates have fallen as the platform became more promotional. InMail now averages $0.40–$1.50 per send, and average response rates have dropped below 10% for most verticals. The platform is saturated with outbound sales messages — decision makers have learned to ignore them.

Cold calls reach gatekeepers more often than decision-makers. Caller ID has made cold calls easy to ignore. The average connect rate for cold calling in 2026 is under 5% for most B2B categories.

The result is that more sales activity is generating fewer qualified conversations. The irony is that most of the businesses sales teams are trying to reach have built a dedicated channel for receiving inquiries — their website contact form. This channel has almost no competition because it requires technology that's only recently become accessible. That's what Loylty provides.

How It Fits

Where Contact Form Sales Outreach Fits in the Stack

Contact form outreach isn't a replacement for cold email, LinkedIn, or phone. It's a complement — a third or fourth channel that reaches the same prospects through a different medium. A prospect who ignored your cold email might notice your contact form inquiry because it arrives in a completely different place: their website notification inbox or staff email. It doesn't look like cold email because it isn't cold email.

The typical sales team using Loylty runs it in parallel with their existing outreach. Here's what a sample weekly rhythm looks like:

Monday
Cold email sequences go out via usual platform. SDR handles morning sequence review.
Tuesday
Loylty review queue — SDR spends 20–30 minutes approving contact form submissions for the day's campaign. Campaign executes automatically.
Wednesday

Loylty campaign continues running automatically at the set daily limit. No additional SDR time required.
Friday

Each additional channel increases the probability that a prospect notices you. Multi-channel outreach outperforms single-channel outreach consistently in every research study on the topic. Contact form is a new dimension of that principle — a channel that reaches the same decision makers through a medium your competitors haven't touched.

What to Say

Message Frameworks That Drive Sales Conversations

The message is where deals are won or lost before they start. These three proven frameworks consistently produce replies from contact form outreach. Each one is under 100 words, specific, and makes a single clear ask.

Framework 1: Pain-Based

Lead with a specific pain point you know this type of business experiences. Follow with evidence that you've solved it for similar businesses. Close with a simple question.

"Most fence contractors I talk to are spending 3–4 hours a week on manual job estimates. We've cut that to under 20 minutes for 40+ contractors in Texas. Worth a quick call to see if it'd work the same for you?"

Why it works: the pain point is specific enough that it signals you actually know their business. The evidence is concrete. The ask is minimal.

Framework 2: Curiosity-Based

Lead with something you observed about their business from their website. Follow with a specific benefit you offer. Close with a low-commitment offer to send more information.

"I noticed you're handling HVAC service for residential clients in the greater Orlando area. We have a scheduling tool that's reduced no-shows by 60% for similar companies. Can I send you a quick overview of how it works?"

Why it works: the opening line shows you looked at their site, not just their category. The ask is "can I send you info" — almost no one says no to that.

Framework 3: Direct Offer

Skip the buildup. State what you do, what the outcome is, and offer a specific low-friction resource. This works for prospects who are too busy to read anything longer than two sentences.

"We help roofing companies win more storm-damage jobs with faster insurance estimates. Our free checklist shows exactly how our top clients handle the first 24 hours after a storm. Want me to send it over?"

Why it works: clear, direct, specific offer. Easy yes. Gets the reply that starts the conversation.

All three frameworks share the same core structure: specificity (they reference the prospect's industry or situation), brevity (under 100 words), a clear single ask (not "let's connect" or "I'd love to chat"), and a real sender identity (name, email, company). These are non-negotiable elements. A message missing any one of them will underperform.

Loylty's AI-personalized mode follows these frameworks by default, adapted to the specific business's website content. The AI reads their site and writes a message that references what they actually do, not just their industry category. This is why AI-personalized consistently outperforms static templates on response rate.
