The Category Explained

What Contact Form Submission Software Does

Contact form submission software automates the process of visiting business websites, finding their contact forms, filling in form fields with your message and contact information, and submitting — at scale. Without software, doing this manually takes 3–5 minutes per business. With Loylty, the mechanical work is fully automated, leaving only the human judgment step: reviewing each prepared submission before it sends.

What specifically gets automated:

  • URL validation and website crawling — Loylty confirms the URL resolves before attempting anything else.
  • Contact page discovery — The crawler navigates from the homepage to the contact page automatically.
  • Form field detection and classification — HTML form elements are identified and labeled by type.
  • Smart field mapping — Your sender data is matched to the correct form fields based on field labels and structure.
  • AI message generation or template insertion — Your message is written per-business or inserted from your template.
  • Rate limiting and pacing — Submissions go out at a controlled cadence, not all at once.
  • Submission execution, status logging, and response tracking — Every submission is recorded with its outcome.

This is fundamentally different from email marketing software, which operates within your own infrastructure. Contact form submission software operates on the open web, visiting real business websites the same way any human visitor would — just faster and at scale. You don't need a domain, an email sending reputation, or a list of opted-in contacts. You need a target niche, a message, and a Loylty account.

The key distinction: Loylty doesn't replace the human judgment call. Every submission is queued for your review before it sends. The software handles the mechanical work; you handle the decision to send.
The Question Everyone Asks

The Difference Between Spam and Legitimate Outreach

This is the question everyone asks, and it deserves a direct answer.

Spam is unsolicited, untargeted, high-volume communication designed to overwhelm the recipient's filtering systems. Spam uses fake sender information, deceptive subject lines, and no mechanism for opting out. Spam is illegal in most jurisdictions under laws like CAN-SPAM, GDPR, and CASL.

Legitimate contact form outreach is different on every dimension:

  • Targeted: You choose specific businesses you want to reach, not random mass blasting. A fence contractor in Texas is a deliberate choice, not a random entry in a harvested list.
  • Identified: Your real name, email, and company are included in every submission. The business can reply, opt out, or ignore — just like any unsolicited business call. There is no hiding behind a fake identity.
  • Human-reviewed: Loylty requires you to review and approve every submission before it sends. You cannot bulk-approve without reading. This isn't just good practice — it's built into the product.
  • Purposeful: You're reaching businesses through the channel they built for exactly this purpose — hearing from people who might want to work with them. The contact form was put there intentionally.

The legal landscape: contact forms are public-facing communication channels. Filling out a contact form is not illegal. It's what the form is for. The relevant legal question is whether the content is deceptive or harassing — not whether it's automated. Loylty's terms of service prohibit deceptive, harassing, or fraudulent outreach. Violators are removed from the platform.

The core test: If you would be embarrassed to have your message shown publicly next to your name and company, don't send it. If it's a genuine, identifiable business inquiry, you're in the clear.
Under the Hood

How Bulk Form Submission Works Technically

Loylty uses Playwright, a browser automation framework, to visit each business website in a real browser environment. This means the crawl looks like real human traffic — not a bot. A real browser renders the page, executes JavaScript, and interacts with the form exactly as a human would. This is why Loylty achieves high submission success rates even on sites with basic bot detection.

Form Detection

Loylty looks for HTML form elements with fields commonly used in contact forms — text inputs for name and email, a textarea for messages, and a submit button. When multiple forms are found on a page, the system identifies the most likely contact form using field count, field labels, and page context. A newsletter signup form and a contact form can both live on the same page; Loylty distinguishes between them based on field structure.

Field Mapping

Once the form is detected, Loylty maps your data to form fields automatically. Name fields get your name. Email fields get your email. The message textarea gets your outreach message. If there's a phone field, your phone number fills it. If there's a company field, your company name fills it. Confidence scoring flags uncertain mappings for human review — so if a field is ambiguous, you'll see it flagged in your review queue rather than having the wrong data submitted silently.

Message Insertion

Depending on your campaign settings, the message is either pulled from your static template, generated by Loylty's AI based on the business's website content, or a hybrid of both. AI-generated messages are unique per business — they reference what the business actually does, rather than sending the same generic text to every form.

What Loylty Does Not Do

Loylty does not bypass CAPTCHA through third-party solvers that violate terms of service. It does not submit to forms on government websites. It does not allow fake sender information. And it does not allow submissions to proceed without human review of at least the first batch of messages in any campaign.

Performance

Submission Success Rates and What Affects Them

Loylty achieves an automated completion rate of 90%+ across campaigns. The 10% that don't complete automatically fall into three categories:

  1. CAPTCHA-protected forms (approximately 5–8%): Google reCAPTCHA v2 and v3, hCaptcha, and similar challenges. Loylty detects these and routes them to a human-assisted flow rather than silently failing.
  2. JavaScript-heavy forms (approximately 2–3%): Multi-step forms, forms with unusual submission logic, or forms embedded in iframes from third-party tools like HubSpot, Typeform, or Gravity Forms. These require additional handling time.
  3. Broken or missing forms (approximately 1–2%): The business's contact page exists but the form is down, the page has an error, or the URL resolves to a 404. These are logged and excluded from your campaign count.

Factors that improve success rate: verified URLs (Loylty validates URLs before processing), clean data (minimal malformed URLs), and targeting businesses with standard website setups rather than complex enterprise sites with custom form infrastructure.

Technical Detail

CAPTCHA Handling

CAPTCHA is the biggest technical challenge in contact form submission. It's a deliberate mechanism designed to prevent automated form interactions. Loylty handles it in three ways, with a strong preference for approaches that don't compromise submission quality.

1. Early Detection

Before attempting submission, Loylty detects whether a CAPTCHA is present on the form. Forms with CAPTCHA are flagged separately in your queue so you have full visibility into what's protected and what isn't. Detection happens during the crawl phase, before any data is filled in.

2. Behavioral Scoring for reCAPTCHA v3

reCAPTCHA v3 is score-based — it doesn't require the user to do anything visible, but it scores the session based on behavior patterns like mouse movement, scrolling, dwell time, and navigation history. Loylty's browser behavior mimics human interaction patterns throughout the session, which typically achieves passing scores without any user intervention. This handles the majority of CAPTCHA-protected forms encountered in campaigns targeting local business websites.

3. Human-Assisted Flow for Visible Challenges

For visible CAPTCHA challenges — the v2 checkbox, image selection puzzles, and audio challenges — Loylty routes these to a human-assisted flow. The CAPTCHA is completed by a human operator before the prepared submission is executed. This adds processing time but ensures the submission is completed correctly. Loylty charges no additional fees for CAPTCHA-assisted submissions.

The result: most CAPTCHA-protected forms are still completable. The presence of CAPTCHA on a business's contact form doesn't mean you can't reach them through Loylty — it just means that specific submission takes slightly longer to process.
