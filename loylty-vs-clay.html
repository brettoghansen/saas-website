Loylty vs Clay: Outreach Tool vs Workflow Automation Platform

Clay and Loylty are solving completely different problems. Clay is a data enrichment and workflow automation platform for building highly personalized outreach lists. Loylty is a contact form outreach tool for reaching those businesses through their own website forms. Comparing them head-to-head misses the point — but understanding how they fit together is genuinely useful.

TL;DR

Clay is a powerful workflow automation platform that pulls data from dozens of sources — Apollo, LinkedIn, Clearbit, Hunter, and 75+ others — to build and enrich prospect lists, score leads, and automate multi-step data workflows. It's complex, technical, and excellent for teams that need highly enriched outreach data. Loylty is a focused execution tool: you bring a list of businesses (or use Loylty's built-in database), and Loylty submits contact form outreach to each one. The two tools are genuinely complementary — Clay builds the enriched list, Loylty contacts them through forms.

Deep Dive

What Clay Actually Does

Clay is a workflow automation platform that connects to 75+ data sources — Apollo, LinkedIn, Hunter.io, Clearbit, People Data Labs, and dozens more. You build "tables" that pull and enrich prospect data from multiple sources simultaneously.

A typical Clay workflow might look like this: pull a list of prospects from Apollo, enrich each one with LinkedIn data to verify job title and company size, find email addresses via Hunter, score each record by firmographic criteria, then automatically push the finished list to your email sequence tool. All without writing code.

Clay's "Claygent" feature uses AI to perform research tasks across the web — checking company websites, summarizing LinkedIn profiles, verifying niche-specific details — at scale. For teams doing highly personalized outreach, this kind of enrichment is what separates a 3% reply rate from a 12% reply rate.

What Clay is genuinely excellent at:

  • Multi-source data enrichment — combining data points from 75+ providers into a single record
  • AI research at scale — Claygent can answer custom questions about each prospect automatically
  • Workflow automation — complex conditional logic, scoring, and routing without engineering resources
  • Integration with every outreach tool — pushes data to email tools, CRMs, LinkedIn tools, and more

Honest limitations: Clay is not an outreach tool. It builds the list and enriches it — it doesn't send anything. The learning curve is real. Understanding Clay's workflow model takes time, and pricing scales significantly with credit usage at high volumes. If you need simple prospecting without complex enrichment, Clay can feel like overkill.

Deep Dive

What Loylty Actually Does

Loylty is a contact form outreach platform. Instead of sending cold emails, it submits outreach messages through the contact forms on business websites — the same form a customer would use to ask a question or request a quote.

Here's how a Loylty campaign works: you select businesses from Loylty's database of 2.3M+ SMBs (filterable by industry, location, and more), or upload your own CSV of business URLs. Loylty's crawler visits each website, detects the contact form, and maps the relevant fields. Its AI generates a personalized outreach message for each business. Before anything sends, every submission goes through a mandatory human review queue — you (or your team) approve each one. Once approved, Loylty submits the form.

What Loylty is genuinely excellent at:

  • Zero spam filter exposure — contact form submissions arrive in the business's website inquiry inbox, not their email inbox
  • No domain infrastructure — no warmup, no sending domains, no reputation management needed
  • Human review before every send — built-in quality control that cold email platforms don't have
  • Built-in SMB database — 2.3M+ businesses, no third-party data source required for getting started
  • Simple setup — no complex workflows; import a list and start reviewing submissions the same day

Honest limitations: Loylty is not a data enrichment tool. It doesn't find individual contact emails, do multi-source enrichment, or run complex scoring workflows. It's a single-channel execution tool — one submission per business, no multi-step sequences. Best suited for SMB and local business targeting rather than enterprise sales where named contact data is critical.

Decision Guide

When to Choose Each Tool

Choose Clay when...

You need to build highly enriched, multi-source prospect lists and automate complex data workflows.

  • You're doing enterprise outreach where individual contact data (emails, titles, company data) matters
  • You need AI research workflows that can answer custom questions about each prospect
  • You're feeding enriched data into email sequences, LinkedIn tools, or a CRM
  • You have technical resources to build and maintain Clay workflows
  • Your outreach quality depends on highly personalized, multi-data-point messaging

Choose Loylty when...

You want to reach businesses through their contact forms — with zero spam filter risk and built-in review.

  • You want to reach businesses through their website contact forms
  • You have a list of business URLs and want to run outreach without building a complex enrichment workflow
  • You're targeting SMBs and local businesses at volume
  • You want zero spam filter exposure regardless of message volume
  • You want mandatory human review before every outreach submission
The strongest case for using both together: Clay to build and enrich your target list — pulling verified website URLs, business names, and niche-specific data from multiple sources — then export to Loylty via CSV and run a contact form campaign against the same businesses you're already reaching through email and LinkedIn. Three channels. One list. Clay feeds all three: your email tool, your LinkedIn tool, and Loylty for contact forms. The teams running outreach at the highest reply rates are the ones not relying on a single channel.
