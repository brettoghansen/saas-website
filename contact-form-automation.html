Let's Be Clear

What Contact Form Automation Actually Means

Contact form automation is not spam. The confusion is understandable — automating any form of outreach at scale sounds like it could cross a line. Here's the distinction:

Spam is untargeted, unsolicited, and designed to deceive. It hides who it's from, uses fake identities, and floods inboxes with irrelevant messages at maximum velocity with no human oversight.

Contact form automation with Loylty is something different in every meaningful way. It's targeted — you choose specific businesses based on real relevance to your offer. It's transparent — your real name, company, and contact information are included in every submission. And it's human-reviewed — every single submission requires your explicit approval before it reaches a real business.

The core principle: Loylty automates the mechanical work — finding the form, mapping the fields, generating a relevant message — not the judgment. You decide what reaches real businesses. The review queue is a mandatory step, not an optional one.

Think of it like having a research assistant who does the legwork of finding contact forms and drafting personalized messages, while you retain final say over what actually gets submitted. The automation handles the repetitive work. The human judgment stays with you.

Time Savings

The Manual vs Automated Workflow

To understand why contact form automation matters, start with what the manual version actually costs in time.

Doing contact form outreach manually means visiting each business website, finding the contact page, filling in your name, email, phone, and message, and clicking submit. For a well-organized person working from a list, this takes 3–5 minutes per business. At that rate:

The automation handles everything except the decision: should this message go out to this business? That decision stays with you. That's the right division of labor — the machine does the repetitive work, the human does the judgment work.

Step by Step

How Loylty's Automation Works

Here's the complete workflow from target list to submitted form:

  1. Import your targets. Upload a CSV of businesses with website URLs, or search Loylty's built-in database of 2.3M+ businesses by industry, city, and state. Column mapping is handled automatically — Loylty identifies business name, URL, location, and other fields from your spreadsheet structure.
  2. Set your template. Write your outreach message template with optional personalization variables. Or switch to AI mode — Loylty reads each business's website and generates a unique, relevant message for each target based on their actual content.
  3. Form detection. Loylty visits each business website using a real browser and locates their contact form. It identifies form fields — name, email, phone, company, subject, message — and maps them to your data. This works even for JavaScript-rendered pages and dynamically loaded forms.
  4. Review queue. Each prepared submission appears in your review queue with the full message, the target business details, and the mapped form fields. You approve or reject. One click either way.
  5. Submission. Approved submissions are sent. Loylty fills the form fields and submits exactly as a human would. The business receives a standard contact form notification from their own website — the same format as any other inquiry they receive.
  6. Tracking. Every submission is logged with timestamp, target business, submission status, and response activity. You have a complete record of what was sent and when.

Protected Forms

CAPTCHA and Protected Forms

Some business websites use CAPTCHA protection on their contact forms — reCAPTCHA, hCaptcha, or similar systems designed to prevent automated submissions. Loylty detects these and handles them transparently.

When a CAPTCHA-protected form is detected, Loylty flags it in your review queue rather than attempting automated submission. You see the target business, the reason for the flag, and your options: skip this business, or submit the form manually by visiting their site directly.

We don't attempt to defeat CAPTCHA protection. This is a principled decision, not a technical limitation. CAPTCHA systems exist for a reason, and bypassing them is not something Loylty does. Businesses that have invested in CAPTCHA protection have opted out of automated submissions, and we respect that.

In practice, this rarely limits your effective reach. The vast majority of small business contact forms — particularly in the home services, professional services, and local business categories that are Loylty's core use case — are not CAPTCHA-protected. Standard HTML forms and common CMS-based contact forms (WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, Webflow) submit without issue in the large majority of cases.

For campaigns targeting larger enterprises or businesses in sectors that commonly use CAPTCHA (financial services, healthcare, government), expect a higher flag rate and plan your target lists accordingly.

Under the Hood

Technical Architecture: Playwright Browser Automation

Loylty's form automation is built on Playwright — an open-source, enterprise-grade browser automation library developed and maintained by Microsoft. It's the same tooling used by engineering teams at major tech companies for end-to-end testing and browser automation at scale.

What this means practically: Loylty interacts with business websites using a real browser engine, not simplified HTTP requests. It opens pages, executes JavaScript, waits for dynamic content to load, and interacts with form elements exactly as a human user would. This is why Loylty works with:

  • JavaScript-rendered contact forms — forms that only appear after page scripts execute
  • Multi-step forms — forms that load additional fields after initial interaction
  • Custom form builders — Gravity Forms, Ninja Forms, Contact Form 7, Typeform embeds, and others
  • Standard CMS forms — WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, Webflow, Shopify, and other common platforms
  • Single-page applications — React, Vue, Angular-based sites where the contact page is rendered client-side

The result is a high form detection and submission success rate across the diverse range of website types you'll encounter when targeting real businesses. Simple HTTP-based tools that don't render JavaScript can't handle this diversity — Loylty can.
