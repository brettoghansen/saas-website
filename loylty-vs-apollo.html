Loylty vs Apollo:
Different Tools for Different Outreach Problems

Apollo is a powerful sales intelligence platform with a built-in email and LinkedIn outreach layer. Loylty is a contact form outreach platform. They're not head-to-head — Apollo is a data and multi-channel outreach platform, Loylty is a specialized channel. Understanding when each wins is the key.

TL;DR

Apollo is a sales intelligence platform with a large B2B contact database, email outreach, LinkedIn automation, and CRM integrations. It's strong for teams that need both data enrichment and multi-channel outreach in one tool, and it scales to enterprise. Loylty is a focused tool that does one thing: reaches businesses through their own contact forms. It doesn't have Apollo's database depth for individual contacts, CRM integrations, or LinkedIn capability — but it has zero spam filter exposure and includes its own business database for SMB targeting.

Deep Dive

What Is Apollo?

Apollo is a comprehensive sales intelligence platform. Its core value is the contact database — 275M+ individual contacts with verified email addresses, job titles, company firmographics, and intent signals. The outreach layer sits on top: email sequences, LinkedIn automation, and CRM sync. Apollo is built for SDR teams and enterprise sales organizations that need verified contact data, multi-channel outreach, and team collaboration in one platform.

The breadth of Apollo's data is its most significant competitive advantage. You can search for contacts by company, job title, industry, company size, geography, funding stage, technology stack, and dozens of other filters. This makes it well-suited for account-based outreach where you need to reach specific people at specific companies — not just companies in general.

Apollo's Strengths

  • Contact-level data at scale. 275M+ verified individual contacts with email addresses, job titles, and intent data. For enterprise ABM outreach, nothing else touches this.
  • Email + LinkedIn in one platform. The ability to run coordinated email and LinkedIn sequences from a single tool reduces the complexity of multi-channel outreach.
  • Advanced filtering and search. Industry, company size, technology used, funding stage, geography, job seniority — Apollo's search layer lets you build precisely targeted lists.
  • Intent data. Apollo surfaces companies that are actively researching topics related to your product, allowing for more timely outreach.
  • CRM sync. Native integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, and Pipedrive push contact and activity data into your pipeline automatically.
  • Team collaboration. Built for SDR teams — sequence templates, shared contact data, team analytics, and admin controls.

Apollo's Honest Limitations

  • Expensive at scale. Per-seat pricing with credits for contact exports adds up quickly for larger teams. Apollo can become a significant line item in your sales stack budget.
  • Still operates in the email layer. Apollo's outreach runs on email and LinkedIn. Both channels face deliverability challenges and inbox competition that Apollo can manage but not eliminate.
  • Not designed for SMB or local business targeting. Apollo's database depth is strongest for named accounts and individual professionals. For reaching local service businesses that don't have LinkedIn-active decision makers, Apollo is less useful.
  • No contact form channel. Apollo has no capability for submitting outreach through website contact forms. It operates exclusively in the email/LinkedIn layer.
  • Complexity overhead. Setup involves domain configuration, CRM connection, sequence building, and team onboarding. The learning curve is real.

What Is Loylty?

Loylty is a contact form outreach platform. It doesn't need individual contact email addresses — instead, it finds the contact form on a business's website and submits your message through it. The message arrives in whatever inbox the business uses to receive website inquiries, bypassing every email spam filter in the process.

Loylty targets businesses as entities, not individuals within them. This makes it fundamentally different from Apollo's contact-centric approach. Where Apollo asks "who at this company should I email?", Loylty asks "what is this company's website contact form, and how do I reach them through it?" The answers lead to very different outreach channels and very different audiences.

Loylty's Strengths

  • Zero spam filter exposure. Contact form submissions route through the target business's own website infrastructure. This is a categorically different channel from email.
  • Built-in business database for SMB outreach. Loylty's 2.3M+ business database is built for targeting companies — local contractors, service businesses, small retailers — not necessarily for finding the Director of IT at a Fortune 500 company.
  • No infrastructure required. No domain purchase, no email warmup, no CRM configuration to start. Upload a CSV or pull from the database and go.
  • Human review queue. Every submission is reviewed before sending. This quality control step produces more careful, intentional outreach.
  • AI personalization per business. Messages are generated for each specific target business, not filled from merge-field templates.
  • Free tier. 100 outreaches included with no credit card — meaningful enough to test a campaign and validate an offer before spending anything.

Loylty's Honest Limitations vs Apollo

  • No individual contact data. Apollo's 275M+ individual contacts with verified emails is a capability Loylty doesn't have and isn't trying to replicate. Loylty targets businesses, not named individuals.
  • No LinkedIn outreach. Apollo's LinkedIn automation is a genuine channel that Loylty doesn't touch.
  • No sequences. Apollo's multi-step sequences across email and LinkedIn have no equivalent in Loylty. One submission per business is the current limit.
  • No CRM integration yet. Apollo's deep CRM sync is a workflow advantage that Loylty's roadmap hasn't reached.
  • SMB and local business focused. Loylty's database and approach are optimized for companies that have website contact forms — which skews toward local and regional businesses rather than enterprise accounts.
Decision Guide

When to Choose Each — and When to Use Both

Apollo and Loylty aren't alternatives. They solve different problems at different market segments. Here's the honest breakdown.

Choose Apollo When...

You're doing enterprise or mid-market ABM and need contact-level intelligence across email and LinkedIn.

  • You're targeting enterprise or mid-market companies
  • You need individual contact data — specific people at specific companies
  • You want email + LinkedIn sequences in one platform
  • You have budget for a premium sales intelligence tool
  • Your targets are at named accounts with LinkedIn-active decision makers
  • You need intent data to prioritize outreach timing

Choose Loylty When...

You're targeting local or regional businesses and want a channel that bypasses every spam filter.

  • You're targeting local or SMB businesses
  • You need zero deliverability risk
  • You don't have individual contact emails — just companies
  • You want to supplement Apollo campaigns with a third touchpoint
  • You're a solo founder or small team without Apollo's budget
  • You want to start the same day without infrastructure setup
The case for using both: Apollo for identifying and contacting key individuals at target accounts via email and LinkedIn. Loylty for reaching the same companies through their contact forms with a different message and sender identity. Three channels (email, LinkedIn, contact form) to the same prospect pool — each one independent, each one adding reach the others can't guarantee.
